How Enforcement Works

1

Identity Verification

User completes KYC via supported provider (identity + sanctions + age).

2

On-Chain Attestation

Provider signs a proof; status is written to the Compliance Registry.

3

Transfer Hook Fires

Every Token-2022 transfer is intercepted and checked against the registry.

4

Approve or Reject

Non-compliant source or destination → transaction fails at runtime.

🔗 Transfer Hook

Automatic interception of every transfer. Validates both source and destination against the on-chain registry.

📋 Compliance Registry

Versioned directory of verified wallets, roles, jurisdiction bitmasks, and policy versions. Zero PII on-chain.

🔐 Permanent Delegate

Secure recovery / clawback path gated by multi-sig or time-lock patterns for institutional control.

📦 TypeScript SDK

High-level client for createCompliantMint, startVerification, isVerified, getOnChainProof, and revoke.
